# Cycle 84 review — narrow Export

Open Export in Fomawrite Dev on a disposable Markdown file. Drag the lower-right resize grip until the dialog is about as narrow as it will go. The three bands should remain side by side, with a horizontal scrollbar below them. Drag that scrollbar from the output settings through styles to the live preview. Check that the title, Cancel and Save stay in place and that the vertical scrollbars still work within output and styles. Expand the dialog again, then drag both band dividers. Report any clipping at your display scale or with a long style name.
